# Portrait credits

Every photograph in this directory is licensed stock from
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license), used as a stand-in for a **fictional**
person in the Thyma prototype.

> **No one pictured is a real Thyma member, patient or clinician, and no one
> pictured has any of the medical conditions named on this site.** The names,
> diagnoses, claims, labs and transcripts attached to these faces are invented
> for demonstration.

If this prototype ever goes in front of an external audience, replace these with
commissioned photography or model-released images cleared for healthcare use.
Standard stock licenses restrict "sensitive use" — depicting an identifiable
model in connection with a medical condition — which is exactly what a patient
persona does.
